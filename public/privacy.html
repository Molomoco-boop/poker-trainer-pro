Privacy Policy — Free Poker Trainer

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Free Poker Trainer is an offline-first poker strategy and math training application.

1. No Account & No Data Collection

No account or login is required. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, analytics, advertising IDs, or location data.

2. Local Device Storage Only

All training progress, rating, hand history, and preferences (language, stakes, audio, deck mode) are stored exclusively in local storage on your device. You can erase all data anytime from Profile → Erase data.

3. No Ads or Trackers

The app contains no advertisements and no third-party tracking SDKs.
