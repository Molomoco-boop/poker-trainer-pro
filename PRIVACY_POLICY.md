# Privacy Policy — Free Poker Trainer

**Last updated:** 2026-11-26

**Free Poker Trainer** ("the App") is an offline-first poker strategy and math training application.

## 1. No Account & No Data Collection
- You do **not** need to create an account or sign in to use the App.
- We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or device identifiers.

## 2. Local Storage Only
- Your training progress, rating, hand review history, and preferences (such as language, stakes, sound, and deck colors) are stored **exclusively on your device** using local storage.
- You can erase all locally stored data at any time from the **Profile → Erase data** screen, or by uninstalling the App.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Ads
- The App contains **no advertisements** and uses **no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs**.

## 4. Children's Privacy
- The App is an educational strategy tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 5. Contact
- For any questions regarding this privacy policy, open an issue in the project repository:
  https://github.com/Molomoco-boop/poker-trainer-pro
